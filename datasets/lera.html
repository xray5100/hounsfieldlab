How to access

Sign in on Redivis, open this dataset, and apply for access. Accept the Stanford research-use terms for this dataset (each dataset is a separate request). Non-commercial research is free. Commercial use needs a separate AIMI license: https://aimi.stanford.edu/datasets-commercial-use

Limitations

Free access is for non-commercial research under a data-use agreement. Do not redistribute the files or the download link. Do not attempt to re-identify anyone. A paid commercial license is a different contract; check the current fee and terms on the AIMI commercial-use page. The Redivis abstract does not state file format or how many studies are normal. Check both on the dataset page before you plan a pipeline.

Citation required?

Yes. Cite the dataset DOI or the citation block on the Redivis page, and name Stanford AIMI.
