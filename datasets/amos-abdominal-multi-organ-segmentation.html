How to access

Open the Zenodo record (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.7262581) and download amos22.zip and the metadata CSV. No account is needed. The record links separate Zenodo records for the unlabeled CT and MRI scans.

Limitations

Labels are organ anatomy (spleen, kidneys, gallbladder, esophagus, liver, stomach, aorta, IVC, pancreas, adrenals, duodenum, bladder, prostate/uterus), not disease, and scans are not labeled normal or abnormal. NIfTI in the nnU-Net folder layout, not DICOM. Built for the MICCAI 2022 AMOS challenge; confirm which splits carry labels before you benchmark. Organ sizes are long-tailed, so small structures such as the adrenals are hard.

Citation required?

Yes. Cite Ji et al., AMOS: A Large-Scale Abdominal Multi-Organ Benchmark for Versatile Medical Image Segmentation, NeurIPS 2022 (arXiv:2206.08023), and the Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.7262581. CC BY 4.0 requires attribution.
