How to access

Open the Zenodo record (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.16970401) and download the archive. No account is required for the public files.

Limitations

Non-commercial and share-alike. Training split of the ISLES 2024 challenge, not the hidden test set. Stroke only. NIfTI in BIDS, with clinical variables, not DICOM. Multimodal series are not a single CT.

Citation required?

Yes. Cite Riedel et al., ISLES'24, Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16970401
