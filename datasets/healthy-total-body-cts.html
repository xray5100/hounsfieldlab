How to access

The CT images can reconstruct a face, so they are not an open download. Follow the NIH Controlled Data Access Policy link on the collection page. Request the images in dbGaP (the policy page lists phs004225, Radiology Imaging Data Containing Faces from The Cancer Imaging Archive, for most TCIA face datasets). The request has to come from someone equivalent to a tenure-track professor or a senior scientist. After approval, create an NCI Data Commons API key and download with the TCIA Data Retriever using the manifest in the Data Access table. Organ segmentations (NIfTI and an organ-values spreadsheet) and the demographics spreadsheet are listed separately under CC BY 4.0 and do not need an application.

Limitations

Do not assume the whole collection is CC BY. Only the segmentations and demographics are CC BY 4.0. The CT requires controlled access. Thirty healthy adults, not a cancer cohort, scanned on the UC Davis uEXPLORER total-body PET/CT system; only the CT is distributed here. The 90-minute CT was about 50 mAs and the other time points about 5 mAs, all at 140 kVp. Each person has three or six same-day time points, stored as one study in version 3. Segmentations cover 37 tissues, were made with MOOSE on the 90-minute scan, and are NIfTI that may need re-orientation to match the DICOM. Version 3 (updated 2026-09-23) corrected NIfTI file names to match the DICOM.

Citation required?

Yes. Cite the collection DOI on the TCIA page (10.7937/nc7z-4f76, version 3) and follow the TCIA Data Usage Policy. Imaging access is governed by the NIH policy, not only by CC BY.
