How to access

Head images can reconstruct a face, so they are not an open download. Follow the NIH Controlled Data Access Policy link on the collection page and complete that request before using the head manifest with the TCIA Data Retriever. Chest, liver, and phantom images, and the clinical spreadsheet, are public Creative Commons rows. Open the collection page and use the download control in the Data Access table (TCIA Data Retriever). Those rows do not need an application. Read the Citations & Data Usage Policy tab before use.

Limitations

Do not assume the whole collection is CC BY. Chest, liver, and phantom images are CC BY 4.0, which allows commercial use with attribution. Head images are under the NIH Controlled Data Access Policy because a face could be reconstructed. The clinical spreadsheet is CC BY 4.0. The Matlab DICOM-CT-PD dictionary and reader on the same table are CC BY 3.0. Version 8 (updated 2026-11-09) corrected 8 chest lesion coordinates in the clinical spreadsheet and clarified the manual; the snapshots were not changed. Very large. Built for reconstruction and denoising research. Each case includes DICOM images and DICOM-CT-PD projection data at the routine dose and at a simulated lower dose (25% for head and abdomen, 10% for chest). Reconstructions are filtered back projection only. The page includes low-dose reconstructed images for the Siemens SOMATOM Definition Flash cases. Scanners named on the page are the Siemens SOMATOM Definition Flash and the GE Lightspeed VCT. These are mixed clinical exams, not a healthy-volunteer set. Check the clinical spreadsheet before treating a case as normal.

Citation required?
