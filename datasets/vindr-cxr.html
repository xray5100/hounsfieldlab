How to access

Create a PhysioNet account, complete CITI “Data or Specimens Only Research” training, and sign the Credentialed Health Data Use Agreement 1.5.0. Then download version 1.0.0 from the Files section.

Limitations

Credentialed research use. You may not share the files or re-identify anyone. Labels are boxes and global findings from 17 radiologists, not full narrative reports. Training labels are three independent readers; the test set is a five-reader consensus.

Citation required?

Yes. Cite the PhysioNet record and the VinDr-CXR paper named on that page. PhysioNet also asks you to contribute code with publications that use the data.
