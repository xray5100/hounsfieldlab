How to access

Open the Zenodo record and download the files. No account is required for the public files. The record names Kitware DicomAnonymizer as the de-identification tool.

Limitations

Stroke cases only, single-center (City Hospital 40, Saint Petersburg). Core and penumbra contours are on slices where the readers marked them. Not a hemorrhage dataset and not a normal-CT dataset.

Citation required?

Yes. Cite Umerenkov, Kudin, Peksheva, and Pavlov, CPAISD, Zenodo, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10892316
