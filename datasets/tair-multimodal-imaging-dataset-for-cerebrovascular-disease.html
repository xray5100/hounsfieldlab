How to access

Install the AWS CLI. No AWS account is needed. Download the zip from the clinical-data page, or run: aws s3 cp s3://multimodal-imaging-for-cerebrovascular-disease/tair-project-data.zip . --no-sign-request . The same bucket can be listed by numeric patient prefix. Allow about 70 GB of disk space. Kitware and UNC-Chapel Hill announced the release on 4 Nov 2026.

Limitations

NIfTI and MP4, not DICOM, so DICOM header metadata is not in this release. CTA and MRA are NIfTI. Fluoroscopy is MP4. The publisher states that the scans were defaced, PHI was removed from metadata, and burned-in text was removed from the fluoroscopy. This is the first of four planned releases, so the counts will change. The first release has no labels or segmentations. The source pages do not say whether any study is free of cerebrovascular disease, so this is not listed as a normal-control set. The clinical-data page describes a permissive license for academic, government, and commercial use. The 4 Nov 2026 Kitware announcement names that license as CC BY 4.0 and says citation is required. Relevant to head and neck CTA work on large-vessel occlusion, aneurysm, and stenosis.

Citation required?

Yes. Lee YZ, de Siqueira Bucci AC, Ho J, Paniagua B, Vicory J, Ravikumar S, Nagler M, Webb JB, Clipp RB. TAIR: Multimodal Imaging Dataset for Cerebrovascular Disease. 2026, v1.0, AWS Registry of Open Data.
