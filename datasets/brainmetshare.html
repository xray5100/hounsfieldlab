How to access

Sign in on Redivis, open this dataset, and apply for access. Accept the Stanford research-use terms for this dataset (each dataset is a separate request). Non-commercial research is free. Commercial use needs a separate AIMI license: https://aimi.stanford.edu/datasets-commercial-use

Limitations

Free access is for non-commercial research under a data-use agreement. Do not redistribute the files or the download link. Do not attempt to re-identify anyone. A paid commercial license is a different contract; check the current fee and terms on the AIMI commercial-use page. Every case has at least one metastasis. Images are skull-stripped and co-registered, not raw DICOM. Confirm the container format (often processed volumes rather than DICOM) on download. 51 cases are held out without public masks.

Citation required?

Yes. DOI 10.71718/z66c-qr59, as printed on the AIMI BrainMetShare page.
