How to access

Search OpenNeuro and open a dataset. Public versions download without a data-use agreement. Read that dataset’s license, README, and version DOI. New uploads can sit in a grace period before they are public.

Limitations

Almost all current releases are CC0, but the license is per dataset and older uploads varied. These are BIDS NIfTI, not clinical DICOM. Many datasets are small research cohorts. Facial features on structural MRI are supposed to be removed; still do not try to re-identify subjects.

Citation required?

Yes. Cite the version DOI. A HowToAcknowledge field, when present, is the authors’ requested wording. CC0 itself does not legally require attribution.

Related open-source tools

  • HD-BET: Deep-learning brain extraction (skull stripping) for MRI
  • FastSurfer: Deep-learning whole-brain segmentation and cortical surface reconstruction, a fast alternative to FreeSurfer recon-all
  • FreeSurfer: Neuroimaging analysis and visualization suite
  • BrainChop: In-browser 3D MRI rendering and segmentation
  • dcm2niix: Converts DICOM to NIfTI with JSON sidecars; the standard converter in many brain MRI and BIDS pipelines
  • TorchIO: Medical image loading, preprocessing and augmentation for PyTorch
  • SimpleITK: Simplified interface to ITK for image I/O, filtering, segmentation and registration in Python, R, Java, C# and other languages
