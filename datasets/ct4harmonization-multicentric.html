How to access

Open the collection page and use the Download control in the Version 2 Data Access table. The page says download requires the TCIA Data Retriever. A public CC BY 4.0 row does not need an application. Read the Citations & Data Usage Policy tab before use. Version 2 was updated 2026-11-20. Data DOI https://doi.org/10.7937/m0pb-bh69 . Preprint https://doi.org/10.48550/ARXIV.2507.01539 . Code for resampling and registration is at https://github.com/medgift/Harmonization-Dataset . The collection page also links https://github.com/QA4IQI/qa4iqi.github.io for loading the raw DICOM.

Limitations

Physical phantom, not patients, so there is no protected health information and no normal human subjects. For scanner harmonization and robustness benchmarking, not clinical diagnosis. Very large download (252.38 GB). Version 2 (2026-11-20) corrected two issues: Center H2 liver segmentations (100 series) were misaligned with the H2 CT images and were regenerated to match that geometry (the H2 CT images were not changed), and missing CT slices were restored in three series (B2_14_FBP#05, 1 slice; D1_1_DL#03, 1 slice; H1_6_IR#04, 6 slices), so each of those series is now complete at 343 slices. Series instance UIDs were changed. Study instance UIDs were not. The Data Access table (1,378 studies and 2,756 series) does not match the methods sentence of 1,378 series from 649 scans, or the abstract's 268 plus 1,378 image series. DICOM files were not preprocessed, so spacing and position still differ by scanner. Scans were repeated at 1, 3, 6, 10, and 14 mGy, with iterative reconstruction, filtered back projection, and, on two scanners, deep-learning reconstruction.
