How to access

Install a BitTorrent client and open the Academic Torrents page for hash 47e9d8aab761e75fd0a81982fa62bddf3a173831. The original headctstudy.qure.ai host is archived; confirm the license text on the torrent page and the archive of the Qure dataset page.

Limitations

Non-commercial and share-alike. Reads are image-level labels, not full narrative reports. The original download site has gone offline, so this torrent is the practical public copy. De-identification was done by the releasing group; do not try to re-identify anyone.

Citation required?

Yes. Cite Chilamkurthy et al., Deep learning algorithms for detection of critical findings in head CT scans, The Lancet 2018, and the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license.
