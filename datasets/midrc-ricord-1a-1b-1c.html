How to access

Start at the 1A page, then download 1B (https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/midrc-ricord-1b/) and 1C (https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/midrc-ricord-1c/) the same way. Open the collection page and use the download control in the Data Access table (TCIA Data Retriever or Aspera, whichever that row names). A public Creative Commons row does not need an application. Read the Citations & Data Usage Policy tab before use.

Limitations

Non-commercial only. 1A is COVID-19 chest CT. 1B adds CT controls. 1C is chest radiographs with COVID and non-COVID labels. These TCIA collections are the RICORD challenge sets, not the whole MIDRC portal. Pixel labels are not full reports.

Citation required?

Yes. Cite the collection DOI on the TCIA page and follow the TCIA Data Usage Policy.
