How to access

Browse collections in the portal. No registration. Files are in Google and AWS public buckets. Filter or inspect the license tag on each file before a commercial project.

Limitations

This is a portal, not one cohort. More than 95% of files are described as CC BY, which allows commercial reuse with attribution, but a subset is CC-NC. De-identification and face-reconstruction risk still apply to head imaging. Not a substitute for reading the license on the files you actually download.

Citation required?

Yes. Cite IDC and the underlying collection.

Related open-source tools

  • HD-BET: Deep-learning brain extraction (skull stripping) for MRI
  • FastSurfer: Deep-learning whole-brain segmentation and cortical surface reconstruction, a fast alternative to FreeSurfer recon-all
  • FreeSurfer: Neuroimaging analysis and visualization suite
  • BrainChop: In-browser 3D MRI rendering and segmentation
  • dcm2niix: Converts DICOM to NIfTI with JSON sidecars; the standard converter in many brain MRI and BIDS pipelines
  • TorchXRayVision: Library of chest X-ray dataset loaders and pretrained classification, segmentation and autoencoder models
  • CheXagent: Foundation model for chest X-ray interpretation
