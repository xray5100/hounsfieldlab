How to access

Read the Dataset Sharing Agreement on the fastMRI page and submit the online application with your institutional affiliation and intended use. Approved users get a personal download link. Each sub-dataset has its own agreement.

Limitations

Internal research or education only. No redistribution, no sharing of the download link, no selling or monetizing, and no attempt to re-identify anyone; the agreement also asks you to destroy copies when your work is done. Most data are raw k-space for reconstruction, not diagnostic labels. DICOM is described for the knee set only. Not for clinical use.

Citation required?

Yes. The agreement requires citing “NYU fastMRI” and Knoll et al., Radiology: Artificial Intelligence 2020 (doi 10.1148/ryai.2020190007), plus arXiv:1811.08839.
