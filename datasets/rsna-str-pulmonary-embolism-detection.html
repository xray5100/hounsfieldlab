How to access

Create a free Kaggle account, open this competition, accept the rules, then download from the Data tab. Do not pass the files to anyone who has not accepted the rules.

Limitations

Competition Data may be used only for the competition, academic research, and education. Commercial use and redistribution are prohibited. These competitions use Kaggle’s standard rules template; re-read this competition’s rules page before you rely on it. Post-challenge copies sometimes add a separate citation line. Study-level PE labels and some image-quality flags. Not full reports. A different, larger PE cohort with reports is INSPECT, under a Stanford agreement.
