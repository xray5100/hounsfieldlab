A Paired Head CT-MRI Dataset for Cross-Modality Image Synthesis

Paired preoperative head CT and MRI from 120 neurosurgical patients (pituitary tumors, meningiomas, vestibular schwannomas, and cerebrovascular disease), released for MRI-to-CT image synthesis and skull-base planning

Size
120 adult patients (74 female, 46 male, ages 18-77): 60 pituitary tumors, 20 meningiomas, 20 vestibular schwannomas, and 20 cerebrovascular diseases. Zenodo v1 lists 40.1 GB: pituitary-tumor.zip 26.7 GB, meningioma.zip 4.5 GB, vestibular-schwannoma.zip 4.5 GB, cerebrovascular-disease.zip 4.4 GB, and participants.xlsx 21.8 kB. CT slice thickness is 0.5-0.625 mm, with a soft kernel (CT-BRAIN) and a sharp kernel (CT-BONE) for every patient. MRI slice thickness is 0.6-1 mm. Contrast-enhanced T1-weighted 3D MPRAGE is included for all 120 patients. Non-contrast T1-weighted MRI is included only for the 60 pituitary-tumor patients.

How to access

Open the Zenodo record and download the four disease zip files plus participants.xlsx. No registration. Version v1 was published 30 October 2025 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17486320; concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17486319). The Scientific Data descriptor, published 25 November 2026, is https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-08901-w .

Limitations

No healthy controls. All 120 patients have one of the four neurosurgical diagnoses. NIfTI only, converted from PACS DICOM with MRIcroGL, so the DICOM headers are not in the release. Single-center study at the First Affiliated Hospital of Sun Yat-sen University. The paper states that participants consented to public release of the de-identified data, that the Ethics Committee of Clinical Research and Animal Trials approved the study [2025(782-1)], and that names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, and acquisition dates were removed from the DICOM metadata before conversion. MRI was N4 bias-corrected with ANTsPy. CT background, including the pillow and the scanner table, was removed. CT and MRI were rigidly registered and resampled to 0.5 mm isotropic. The paper states that faces were defaced with PyDeface, using the mask from the contrast-enhanced T1-weighted scan on the other series. The Zenodo preview of cerebrovascular-disease.zip contains macOS __MACOSX/._ metadata files and a .DS_Store that can be ignored. MRI is Siemens 3T 3D MPRAGE only. There is no T2 or FLAIR. CT scanners were Toshiba, GE, Siemens, and Philips. Intended for MRI-to-CT synthesis and for skull-base and neurosurgical planning. The Scientific Data article is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; that is the article license, not the dataset license.
