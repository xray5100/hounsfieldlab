How to access

Open the collection page and use the download control in the Data Access table (TCIA Data Retriever or Aspera, whichever that row names). A public Creative Commons row does not need an application. Read the Citations & Data Usage Policy tab before use. Images and segmentations are one NIfTI package, and that row requires the IBM Aspera Connect plugin. Clinical, treatment, imaging-availability, RANO, and PyRadiomics TSVs are separate small downloads on the same table.

Limitations

NIfTI only, and skull-stripped. DICOM is deliberately not provided, to avoid leakage of header metadata. MRI brain extraction used ANTsPyNet on the pre-treatment post-contrast T1, and the other MRI series were rigidly registered to that scan. Planning CT was defaced with TotalSegmentator (thermoplastic mask removal) and then CTA-DEFACE. Not healthy controls: 264 deceased adults with glioblastoma, treated with the Stupp protocol at one center (Centre François Baclesse) from November 2017 to December 2023, and not enrolled in a trial. Not every sequence or time point exists for every patient (planning CT 195, RTDOSE 194; tumor masks follow the post-contrast T1 exams). GTV contours are from routine radiotherapy planning. Clinical, treatment, and RANO TSVs are included, plus a PyRadiomics feature table. Version 4 (updated 2026-09-11) corrected the RANO TSV to enhancing-tumor measurements and renumbered some folders; the images were not changed.

Citation required?

Yes. Cite the collection DOI on the TCIA page (10.7937/v9pn-2f72, version 4) and follow the TCIA Data Usage Policy.
