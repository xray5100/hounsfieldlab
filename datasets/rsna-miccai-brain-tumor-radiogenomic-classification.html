How to access

Create a free Kaggle account, open this competition, accept the rules, then download from the Data tab. Do not pass the files to anyone who has not accepted the rules.

Limitations

Competition Data may be used only for the competition, academic research, and education. Commercial use and redistribution are prohibited. These competitions use Kaggle’s standard rules template; re-read this competition’s rules page before you rely on it. Post-challenge copies sometimes add a separate citation line. Glioma patients only. The task is a radiogenomic label, not tumor segmentation.

Citation required?

Yes. Cite the RSNA-MICCAI brain tumor competition and its dataset paper. Check the rules page for the requested wording.
