How to access

Open the Zenodo record and download the patient zip files plus the clinical/demographic spreadsheet and the MRI radiomics spreadsheet. No registration. Version v5 was published 5 October 2026 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23171182; concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.15499111). Radiomics code is at https://github.com/InSilicoModellingGroup/pyRadiomicBM .

Limitations

No healthy controls. All 40 patients had brain metastases, identified at the Bank of Cyprus Oncology Centre and imaged at six diagnostic centers in Cyprus (University of Cyprus and Bank of Cyprus Oncology Centre). The Scientific Data data record says the raw pre-treatment and follow-up scans are DICOM, and that BraTS-space MRI (T1, T2, post-contrast T1, and FLAIR), the planning CT, the radiotherapy plan, and the segmentations are NIfTI. Segmentations of 65 metastases cover enhancing tumor, edema, and necrotic core. The same mask files also label ventricles, white matter, grey matter, and CSF. The paper's data-availability link is an earlier Zenodo version (10.5281/zenodo.17253793); this row uses the current v5 record. Folder names may include exam dates. The paper states that DICOM headers were de-identified with the Eclipse anonymizer before deposit. The clinical spreadsheet includes dates of diagnosis, lesion detection, and treatment. The Scientific Data article is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; that is the article license, not the dataset license.

Citation required?

Yes. Cite Flouri et al., A longitudinal MRI dataset of brain metastases with tumor segmentations, clinical and radiomic data, Scientific Data, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-06131-0 , and the Zenodo version DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23171182. CC BY 4.0 requires attribution.
