Featured: Pillar-0

An open radiology foundation model from YalaLab. Pretrained on 42,990 abdomen-pelvis CTs, 86,411 chest CTs, 14,348 head CTs and 11,543 breast MRIs, with RATE, an LLM-based framework that extracts labels for 366 radiologic findings from reports. The authors release code, weights and evaluation tools.
