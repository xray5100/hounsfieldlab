DICOM datasets

68 datasets · 39 open download · 32 with commercial-use licenses · Last updated 26 Oct 2026

A directory of public DICOM, and a few closely related NIfTI, collections for radiologists and researchers training imaging models.

Each card is a link to the source download, plus the license, who can get in, and whether the set includes normals. Hounsfield Lab does not host images. The checked date is the day we loaded the source page. Files and terms change after that. This is not legal advice.

How to read a license

  • CC BY (3.0 or 4.0) lets you copy, adapt, and use the data commercially if you give the credit the license asks for.
  • CC BY-SA adds share-alike. If you redistribute an adaptation of the dataset, that adaptation stays under the same license.
  • CC BY-NC and CC BY-NC-SA are non-commercial. Those cards carry a red Non-commercial badge. Training a product, or using the data inside a paid service, is usually outside the grant even when the work happens in a lab. Read the deed.
  • Data-use agreements (PhysioNet, MIDRC, Stanford AIMI, NIH controlled access, Kaggle competition rules) are contracts. They often require an account, forbid redistribution, forbid re-identification, and limit you to research or education. Some hosts sell a separate commercial license. A DUA is not a Creative Commons license.
  • De-identification is not permission. A dataset can be de-identified and still be off limits for your use. Head CT and MRI can sometimes be turned back into a face; several brain collections are controlled for that reason. Do not try to re-identify anyone.
  • Rows marked Check license or Check source are ones where the public page did not give a single clear grant. Use the source text, not this summary.

Unverified suggestions

Ideas sent through the form are not published by themselves. If we add one before checking the source, the card gets an orange badge and this warning: “UNVERIFIED — community-submitted, not reviewed by Hounsfield Lab; verify license and de-identification yourself.” We never host DICOM files or protected health information. Please don’t send either.

Commercial OKVerifiedOpen download

Multi-center abdominal CT and MRI from patients with a range of diseases, with voxel labels for 15 abdominal organs

Size. 500 CT and 100 MRI scans (240 train, 120 validation, 240 test); one 24.2 GB zip

AREABodyMODCT, MRFORMATNIfTINORMALSPartialLICCC BY 4.0CHECKED2026-10-26

How to access

Open the Zenodo record (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.7262581) and download amos22.zip and the metadata CSV. No account is needed. The record links separate Zenodo records for the unlabeled CT and MRI scans.

Limitations

Labels are organ anatomy (spleen, kidneys, gallbladder, esophagus, liver, stomach, aorta, IVC, pancreas, adrenals, duodenum, bladder, prostate/uterus), not disease, and scans are not labeled normal or abnormal. Citation. Yes. Cite Ji et al., AMOS: A Large-Scale Abdominal Multi-Organ Benchmark for Versatile Medical Image Segmentation, NeurIPS 2022 (arXiv:2206.08023), and the Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.7262581. CC BY 4.0 requires attribution. Added 26 Oct 2026.
