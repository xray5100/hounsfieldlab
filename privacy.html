Privacy notice

Last updated: October 14, 2026

What we collect

This site has no accounts, cookies, or analytics. We only receive information you choose to send through the Contact or Suggest a tool forms: the fields you fill in (for example name, email, message, tool name and URL).

How forms are processed

Form submissions are delivered to us by email through FormSubmit, a third-party form service, which processes the data to deliver it. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations.

How we use it

We use submissions only to review tool suggestions and corrections and to reply to you. We don’t sell your information or add you to any mailing list. The newsletter isn’t live yet.

Retention and your choices

We keep submissions only as long as needed to handle them. To ask us to delete something you sent, use the contact form.

Please don’t send patient information

These forms aren’t for protected health information or any identifiable patient data.
