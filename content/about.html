About Hounsfield Lab

Hounsfield Lab is a directory of open-source and downloadable software for medical AI development, curated by a practicing neuroradiologist. It covers radiology first, plus pathology, clinical NLP and EHR tooling.

How we list tools

  • Every entry links to its public repository or official download.
  • License and last-update dates come from GitHub repository metadata (snapshot: Oct 13, 2026).
  • Maintained means the repo was updated within the last 12 months. Stale means it wasn’t.
  • COMM summarizes the license we read by hand: Permissive, Copyleft (GPL/AGPL), Non-commercial, or Verify (custom terms or separate model-weight terms). It is not legal advice.

Imaging datasets are a separate directory: DICOM datasets. Those rows record license, access, and whether normals are included. They are links to the source, not files we host.

What we are not

Listings aren’t endorsements, medical advice, or claims of regulatory clearance. Most tools here are for research use.
