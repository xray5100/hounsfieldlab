HoloCT

3D CT foundation model that takes a CT volume (NIfTI, Hounsfield units) and a text prompt for lesion screening, VQA, report generation and language-guided segmentation, returning 3D masks as evidence for its findings; HoloCT-4B and HoloCT-8B checkpoints on Hugging Face (Nov 2026)

THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES lists components from Med3DVLM (MIT), MONAI VISTA (Apache-2.0) and Transformers/Qwen3-VL (Apache-2.0). Training data (HoloDATA) draws on 73 public datasets with their own terms plus clinical cohorts. Needs an NVIDIA GPU with bfloat16. Paper: arXiv 2611.06471 (Nov 4, 2026).
