SlimFormer++

Lightweight (5.98M-parameter) 3D segmentation network packaged as an nnU-Net v2 trainer, with pretrained weights for ACDC (cardiac MRI), AbdomenCT-1K and AMOS2022 CT and a 3D Slicer extension for 15 abdominal organs in CT
