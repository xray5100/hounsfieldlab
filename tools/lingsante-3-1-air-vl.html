LingSante-3.1-air-VL

Natively multimodal mixture-of-experts vision-language model (124B total parameters, 5.5B active per token, 256K context) built on Ling-3.0-flash-VL, for medical imaging reports (for example chest X-ray findings and impression), multimodal health assessment, medical search and evidence synthesis, and medical GUI agents; research model, not a medical device. Recommended serving is SGLang on 4× 141GB-class GPUs (TP4) or 8× 80GB H100s (TP8); a vLLM fork is also available
