IMPACT-Seg

Segments 126 anatomical structures (organs, lung lobes, heart chambers and great vessels, every vertebra and rib by level, bones, trunk muscles and head-and-neck organs at risk) with the same network on planning CT, cone-beam CT and MRI, with no modality flag; 2.5D and 3D residual-encoder U-Nets (five folds each) plus a coarse body-region model, trained on 8,290 harmonized CT, real and simulated CBCT and MRI volumes; its decoder features also serve as a multimodal registration similarity. Runs as KonfAI apps (pip install impact_seg_konfai) or a 3D Slicer extension
