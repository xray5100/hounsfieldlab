CXAS

Chest X-ray anatomy segmentation (Mask2Former models: 187 frontal and 177 lateral classes) plus 132 named landmarks and landmark-based measurements such as cardiothoracic ratio, costophrenic angle, Cobb angle and the Haller index, in mm for DICOM input; cxas_idc writes one multi-segment DICOM SEG and a TID 1500 SR per radiograph; trained on PAXRay++ CT-projected radiographs

License review: Repo LICENSE.txt is MIT, and the README states MIT for code and model weights. Weights are public, not gated, on Hugging Face (cmseibold/cxas-models and cmseibold/cxr-landmarks, both tagged MIT) and are tag-pinned and SHA-256 checked on download. v0.1.0 (Nov 13, 2026) added the new models, landmarks and DICOM SEG/SR output; v0.1.3 (Nov 14) fixed landmark measurements under NumPy 2. As of Nov 19, PyPI lists 0.1.2 as the latest version; the 0.1.3 Docker images pin NumPy 1.26. The README states it is a research tool, not a medical device. Papers: BMVC 2022 (PAXRay) and arXiv 2306.03934.

Related tools in Segmentation & frameworks

  • MONAI: PyTorch-based AI toolkit for healthcare imaging
  • MONAI Label: AI-assisted image labeling and active learning; integrates with 3D Slicer and OHIF
  • nnU-Net: Self-configuring segmentation framework
  • TotalSegmentator: Segments more than 100 anatomical structures in CT and MR
  • TotalSpineSeg: Two-stage nnU-Net tool that segments and individually labels every vertebra and intervertebral disc plus the spinal cord and canal, using an odd/even vertebra scheme and landmark discs (C2-C3, C7-T1, T12-L1, L5-S) to avoid fused or split vertebrae; works on localizers and short-FOV scans; peer-reviewed in European Radiology (Nov 7, 2026)
  • IMPACT-Seg: Segments 126 anatomical structures (organs, lung lobes, heart chambers and great vessels, every vertebra and rib by level, bones, trunk muscles and head-and-neck organs at risk) with the same network on planning CT, cone-beam CT and MRI, with no modality flag; 2.5D and 3D residual-encoder U-Nets (five folds each) plus a coarse body-region model, trained on 8,290 harmonized CT, real and simulated CBCT and MRI volumes; its decoder features also serve as a multimodal registration similarity. Runs as KonfAI apps (pip install impact_seg_konfai) or a 3D Slicer extension
