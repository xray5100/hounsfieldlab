Related tools in Segmentation & frameworks

  • MONAI: PyTorch-based AI toolkit for healthcare imaging
  • MONAI Label: AI-assisted image labeling and active learning; integrates with 3D Slicer and OHIF
  • nnU-Net: Self-configuring segmentation framework
  • TotalSegmentator: Segments more than 100 anatomical structures in CT and MR
  • NV-Segment-CT: CT segmentation built from MONAI VISTA3D: 117 structures automatically, or 132 promptable by point or class. Research use only, not for clinical care. Needs a CUDA GPU; weights are about 872 MB
  • MedSAM: Segment Anything adapted for medical images
